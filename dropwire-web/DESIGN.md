# DropWire Design System & Decisions

## Brand Identity
- **Hero Object:** 3D Brand Cube (Gold top, Lavender side, Cream front, Charcoal `t`).
- **Color Palette:**
  - Ink Background: `#0B1016`
  - Ink Elevated Surface: `#161D24`
  - Gold Accent / Primary CTA: `#E1B865` (`#F3D578` bright glow)
  - Purple Secondary Accent: `#AA90B3` (`#705090` deep)
  - Cream Text & Highlights: `#FEF7E4`
  - Ochre Mid Dividers: `#7C6542`

## Approved Typography
- **Headlines & Display:** Cabinet Grotesk (Variable)
- **Body & Interface:** Satoshi (Variable)
- **Code & Terminal:** JetBrains Mono

## Motion System
- **Smooth Scroll:** Lenis (`lenis`) with `prefers-reduced-motion` safety.
- **3D Tilt:** Atropos (`atropos`) for hero cube card interaction.
- **Scroll Reveals:** Motion / Framer Motion (`framer-motion`) CSS transitions.
