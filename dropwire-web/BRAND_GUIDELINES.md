# DropWire Visual Design & Brand Guidelines
*Reference document for AI Image Generation and UI Extension*

This document outlines the exact typography, color palettes, layout structures, and aesthetic vibe of the DropWire website. Use these descriptions as prompts or reference material when generating AI assets (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E) or extending the website.

---

## 🎨 1. Core Aesthetic & Vibe

- **Style Keywords**: Warm editorial, Neo-Brutalism, Awwwards-winning design, ultra-minimalist, high-end typography, Swiss design, flat architectural geometry.
- **Vibe**: It feels like a high-end luxury print magazine met a hardcore cyberpunk protocol. It relies on massive, heavy typography contrasting against vast amounts of warm, empty cream space, punctuated by extremely saturated, electric color blocks.
- **Lighting & Textures**: No heavy shadows, no complex gradients. The design relies on flat, solid colors, clean grid lines, and subtle thin borders.

---

## 🖌️ 2. Color Palette

The color system relies on a very neutral canvas with deep, high-contrast ink, punctuated by neon/electric accents.

### Canvas & Neutral Backgrounds
- **Warm Editorial Cream (Main Background)**: `#F4EFEA`
- **Pure White (Card Backgrounds)**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Sand / Grid Borders**: `#EBE5DC` to `#D9D2C9`

### Ink & Typography
- **Deep Charcoal Black (Primary Text)**: `#0B1016`
- **Soft Ink (Secondary Text)**: `#222C37`

### Electric Accent Colors (Used for pills, hover states, and mosaic pixels)
- **Electric Blue**: `#0052FF`
- **Solar Yellow**: `#FFB800`
- **Crimson Orange**: `#FF3D00`
- **Emerald Green**: `#00B060`
- **Deep Purple**: `#9D00FF` / `#AB54F7`

*(**AI Prompt Tip**: "Warm editorial cream background `#F4EFEA` with flat electric blue and solar yellow accents, neo-brutalist graphic design")*

## 🔤 3. Typography System

The website relies on three highly specific fonts to achieve its look. 

1. **Display / Headline Font: `Alfabet` (Black 850 Weight)**
   - **Usage**: Used for the giant, massive headlines (e.g., `DropWire.`).
   - **Styling**: Always rendered in ultra-heavy weight, usually ALL-CAPS, with extremely tight letter spacing (`tracking-tighter`). It sits flush and heavy on the page.

2. **Body / Sans Font: `Aeonik Pro` (Regular 400 & Bold 700)**
   - **Usage**: Used for paragraphs, standard UI text, and descriptions.
   - **Styling**: Clean, geometric, and highly readable.

3. **Technical / Monospace Font: `JetBrains Mono`**
   - **Usage**: Used for small technical labels, sub-headers (e.g., `P2P ENCRYPTED ENGINE`), and status badges.
   - **Styling**: Always rendered quite small (`text-xs` or `text-sm`), ALL-CAPS, heavily bolded, and with very wide letter spacing (`tracking-[0.25em]`).

*(**AI Prompt Tip**: "Massive ultra-heavy bold sans-serif typography, tight letter spacing, contrasting with small wide-tracked monospace tech labels")*

---

## 📐 4. UI Geometry & Layout

- **Sharp vs. Curved**: The design uses a mix of flat, perfectly sharp elements (like the mosaic square pixels `rounded-none`) and deliberate, soft curves for cards (`rounded-xl` or `rounded-[2.5rem]` for large containers).
- **Borders**: Elements are often separated by very thin, subtle lines (`border-[#D9D2C9]`) rather than drop shadows.
- **Grid Systems**: Strict architectural grids are highly prominent. The footer uses a perfect square matrix grid where lines intersect perfectly.
